# Media plan

Based on "Tuning in to information flows".

The plan has two parts:

1. **The grid** lists every media domain in four ways: at home (recorded), live (you attend), take part (you do it), and create (you make something from it). The grid is the completeness check.
2. **The checklist** puts the grid into a Monday to Sunday plan, and then monthly, quarterly and yearly slots. Weekdays have a limit of 60 minutes of media each day.

**Create while you consume.** You make something in the same slot in which you consume. Creation is part of the slot time, not extra time.

- **Two panes:** the media on one side, and your editor or sketchbook on the other side. On a phone or a tablet, use split view.
- **Write as you go:** write your notes in the form of the final piece (a review paragraph, a caption, a reply), not as raw notes for later.
- **Pause to draw:** when a frame, a figure or a scene catches you, pause and sketch it before you continue.
- **Finish on the weekend:** the weekend slot only finishes and publishes what you started during the week.

| Mode | Create while you consume |
|---|---|
| K-drama, show, film | Write the review with timestamps as you watch. One paragraph for each act or each 15 minutes. |
| Anime | Pause on one frame for each episode and draw it (a 5-minute gesture sketch). These become your fan art. |
| Hacker News, RSS, tweets | Clip each good comment or post with your one-line view as you read. The clip list is the "This week's finds" draft. |
| Articles, papers | Annotate in the margin, and write the 3-sentence summary before you close the page. Sketch the key figure. |
| Music | Write one line for each song while it plays, and add the song to the playlist at once. |
| Podcasts, lectures | Write timestamped notes, and one quote to share. |
| Games | Take a screenshot with a caption at each moment that you want to talk about. |
| Live events | Sketch or photograph on the spot, and write 3 lines before you leave. |
| Books | Write a commonplace note at the end of each session. |

At the end of each year, each cell of the grid must have at least one tick. If a domain is missing from the grid, add a row.

**Post as you go (in the style of @jwt0625, "outside five sigma").** That account posts what it reads and uses in its own fields (photonics, RF, nanofabrication, robotics): papers, standards, product pages, videos and old articles, with a short title and a link, and sometimes one line of opinion. Do the same, but across all the domains of the grid.

- **One post for each find, at the moment you find it.** This is the "create while you consume" step in its smallest form.
- **The format:** `<Year> - <Title>` and a link, for example "1987 - IEEE Standard on Piezoelectricity". For a paper: `<Team or author> <Year>: <title>`, the link, and the arXiv link. Add one line of your own view when you have one. A video clip or a sketch is a post too.
- **Old sources:** at least one post each week is an old source: a paper, a standard, a manual, a magazine article or a filing that is more than 20 years old (for example from archive.org, Bitsavers or an old journal issue).
- **Your own work:** post what you build (femahoy, Toybox, the theorems pages) with a clip or a picture, and link the code.
- **Short thoughts:** a single sentence that you thought of is a post too.
- **Spread:** add the grid domain number to each post in your own log. On Sunday, check that the week's posts cover at least 4 domains.
- **One home:** post on X, and keep a copy of each post in a log page on your website.

## 2. The checklist

### Weekly plan (Monday to Sunday)

Weekdays have a hard limit of 60 minutes of media each day. Each weekday has a core of 35 minutes and one theme slot of 25 minutes. You create during the theme slot, in the same 25 minutes. The weekend holds the longer items and the monthly slot.

**Weekday core (35 minutes, Monday to Friday)**

- [ ] Text (1): news and blogs from one RSS reader, 10 minutes. No news outside this slot.
- [ ] Text (1): textbook, one section or one exercise, 15 minutes.
- [ ] Text (1): fiction or history, 10 minutes before sleep.
- [ ] People (17): ask one person one real question, and write one line about the answer. This is a conversation, not media time.

**Weekday theme slot (25 minutes, create while you consume)**

| Day | Theme slot | What you create in the slot |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Text (1): essays, newsletters and saved articles. Skip the ones that no longer seem interesting. | One quote or idea for "This week's finds". |
| Tuesday | Music (2): the top 10 of one country's chart, with its music videos. Use a different country each week. | The best song, with one line on why, for the playlist. |
| Wednesday | Text (1): one journal paper (once a month, an old paper or standard of more than 20 years). Read the abstract, the figures and the conclusion first. | A summary of the paper in 3 sentences. |
| Thursday | Film and video (5): one anime episode. | A gesture sketch of one frame, drawn on pause. It becomes fan art. |
| Friday | Online communities (16): forums and social text, 15 minutes, timed. Then Film and video (5): short video from a region or a language that you do not know, 10 minutes, timed. | The best Hacker News comment, RSS post or tweet, with one line of your own view. |

**Saturday (about 2 hours, plus the monthly slot)**

- [ ] Music (2): Malay, Tamil or Chinese radio, 20 minutes (for example during errands).
- [ ] Film and video (5): one drama episode.
- [ ] Film and video (5): one long-form YouTube video or video essay.
- [ ] Games (7): puzzles (crossword, logic puzzle or chess problem), 20 minutes.
- [ ] Text (1): one poem, read aloud.
- [ ] Finish (45 minutes): finish one piece that you started during the week. Rotate by week of the month:
  - Week 1: the K-drama review that you wrote while you watched the Saturday drama episode.
  - Week 2: anime fan art, from the frames that you drew on pause.
  - Week 3: a review or an explainer of the film, game, paper or filing of the month.
  - Week 4: a free choice from the Create column, in the month's spotlight domain.
- [ ] Monthly slot: see the table below.

**Sunday (about 2 hours, plus the monthly slot)**

- [ ] Spoken word (3): one podcast episode.
- [ ] Film and video (5): one show episode.
- [ ] Text (1): fiction or history, 30 minutes.
- [ ] Publish "This week's finds" (30 minutes): the best Hacker News comments, RSS posts and tweets from the clips that you made while you read, each with one line of your own view. End with the surprise of the week and the grid cell that it came from. This replaces the separate surprise log.
- [ ] Plan next week: choose the chart country, and the papers and articles for Monday and Wednesday.
- [ ] Monthly slot: see the table below.

**Monthly slot (by week of the month)**

| Week | Saturday | Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Live event 1 | Film 1, and one full album |
| 2 | Take-part activity | One primary document, and one lecture or talk |
| 3 | Live event 2, and one meal from a cuisine that you do not know | Film 2 |
| 4 | Club or meetup, or one video game or board game | Documentary, comics, photobook or maps, one critic essay, the monthly review, and publish the best Make piece of the month |
| 5 (when the month has one) | Spotlight domain or catch-up | A quarterly item |

### Monthly

The weekend monthly slot holds these items.


- [ ] Live: 2 live events from the Live column. Use a different domain each time, and do not repeat a domain in the same quarter.
- [ ] Take part: 1 activity from the Take part column. Use a different domain each month.
- [ ] Spotlight: choose one domain for the month and go deeper at home in it.
- [ ] Text (1): one volume of comics, manga or webtoon.
- [ ] Text (1): one essay from a critic of popular culture, for example Ted Gioia or Venkatesh Rao.
- [ ] Music (2): one full album, in sequence, from a genre that you do not know.
- [ ] Spoken word (3): one lecture or talk.
- [ ] Art and image (4): one photobook, or maps and data visualisation.
- [ ] Film and video (5): 2 films (one classic, and one current film from another country).
- [ ] Film and video (5): one documentary.
- [ ] Games (7): one video game in a genre that you do not play, or one board game or card game.
- [ ] Food and drink (9): one meal from a cuisine that you do not know.
- [ ] Places and architecture (11): look closely at one building or one street.
- [ ] Civic and commerce (15): one primary document (a 10-K, a law, a patent or a standard). For a 10-K, read Items 1, 1A and 7, and compare them with last year's report.
- [ ] People (17): one club, meetup or class.
- [ ] Review: re-read the month's notes. Keep only what you thought about again, and delete the rest.
- [ ] Review: delete one news source if you could predict its stance before you read it.

### Quarterly

- [ ] Text (1): one book of letters, diaries or memoirs.
- [ ] Text (1): one translated work from a language that you do not read.
- [ ] Text (1): finish one set of textbook chapters, and write a one-page summary in your own words.
- [ ] Music (2): opera, or classical music from a tradition that is not Western.
- [ ] Spoken word (3): oral history, field recordings or soundscapes.
- [ ] Film and video (5): one livestream, or animation that is not anime.
- [ ] Performance (6): one recorded theatre or dance work.
- [ ] Games (7): interactive fiction, a simulation, or a tabletop role-playing game session.
- [ ] Sport (8): watch one full match of a sport that you do not follow.
- [ ] Nature (12): one nature documentary or field guide.
- [ ] Science and technology (13): one explainer series or one paper outside your field.
- [ ] Make: publish one piece about something that you read, watched or attended this quarter.
- [ ] Make: make one thing in a domain from the grid (a photo series, a song cover, a sketch, a short video). Use a different domain each quarter.
- [ ] Review: read the surprise log. If most of the surprises came from one domain or one culture, change next quarter's live events and chart countries. Give less time to a cell that gave no surprise for two quarters.
- [ ] Review: look at the shelf list, and add or remove books.

### Yearly

- [ ] Grid check: each of the 68 cells has at least one tick. Plan the empty cells into next year's monthly slots.
- [ ] Text (1): about 12 history books and 12 novels from the shelf list, one of each in turn, and 2 plays or screenplays.
- [ ] Ritual and faith (14): services or festivals of at least 2 faiths that are not your own.
- [ ] Design, fashion and craft (10): one design week, fashion show or craft market.
- [ ] Culture: one longer contact with a culture that you do not know (a trip, a course, or all the albums of one artist).
- [ ] People (17): one long interview with an older person, recorded as oral history.
- [ ] Make: one essay that updates "Tuning in to information flows": what you tuned in to, what you tuned out, and what the surprise log showed.
- [ ] Rest: one break of a few days with no devices. This is a break, not a way to live.
